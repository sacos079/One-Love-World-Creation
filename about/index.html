About One Luv

A Miami family business, built by word of mouth.

It started with customizing pieces for people in our community — a name on a jacket, a patch on a vest, a matching set for a family reunion. People wore them, people asked, and it grew from there.

Valerie handles design and production. Sebastian runs the operations and business side. Every order that comes through is handled personally, by us.

We grew by word of mouth because we care about every piece we make. That hasn't changed.
